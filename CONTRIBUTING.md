# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve the Management OS plugin, skills and docs.

**Issues are welcome.** Report a bug, a broken link or a step that doesn't work, and suggest improvements.

**Pull requests are not accepted.** Management OS maintains these files so they stay consistent with Management OS and the methodology behind it.

**Never include customer data.** Issues are public. Leave out CRM records, call notes, emails and anything that identifies a customer, a deal or a person.

**Security problems** go through our [security policy](https://github.com/Management-OS-Inc/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md), not public issues.

For help using Management OS, see [SUPPORT.md](https://github.com/Management-OS-Inc/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
